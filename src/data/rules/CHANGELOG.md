# Rules Changelog (Chicago / Cook County / Illinois) — last 5 years

This changelog tracks *rule artifacts used by Building Ledger* (not every housing law change).
Dates reflect when a rule artifact is known to have changed or been (re)published.

## 2021
- **Cook County Residential Tenant Landlord Ordinance (RTLO)** took effect (June 1, 2021). (Cook County applies outside the City of Chicago; this repo currently tracks Cook County eviction execution rules separately.)
- **Chicago security deposit interest rate** published as **0.01%** for 2021.

## 2022
- Chicago security deposit interest rate published as **0.01%** for 2022.

## 2023
- Chicago security deposit interest rate published as **0.01%** for 2023.

## 2024
- City of Chicago indicates **RLTO Summary effective as of May 1, 2024** (PDF links posted on the City RLTO page).
- Chicago security deposit interest rate published as **0.01%** for 2024.

## 2025
- Chicago security deposit interest rate published as **0.01%** for 2025.

## 2026
- Chicago security deposit interest rate published as **0.01%** for 2026.
- Cook County Sheriff guidance continues to state the Sheriff is the only entity that may evict a tenant.
- **2026-06-29:** Chicago **Protecting Renters Ordinance (PRO)** introduced. It would amend most of the RLTO. Tracked as `proposed` versions in `chicago/rlto_provisions.json` and in `proposals.json`. Not law.
- **2026-07-15:** Alternative **FAIR** ordinance introduced. Tracked in `proposals.json`. Not law.
- **2026-09-16:** Housing Committee advanced a PRO substitute (12-9). Full Council vote deferred on 2026-09-23 and 2026-09-29. Next meeting 2026-10-14.
- **2026-10-07 (repo review):**
  - RLTO summary link now points to the City's current summary (revised December 2023, in effect since May 1, 2024), in English and Spanish. The old `RLTOEnglish.pdf` link was a 2011 scan.
  - Added `chicago/rlto_provisions.json` with cited RLTO rules: notice address (5-12-090), 14-day repair request (5-12-110), essential services 24/72 hours (5-12-110(f)), entry notice (5-12-050), retaliation (5-12-150), deposit return (5-12-080), lockouts (5-12-160).
  - Heat rule: added the City's exceptions for buildings with one heat-and-cooling system and for units with their own heater. Fixed the City heat page URL.
